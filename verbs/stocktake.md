# stocktake — every open line, at source

An add-on no rule reads (specification 3.22), for the owner's request
to take stock of the backlog and wish list, before a plan. Lab-only
until his word. It grants no gate, research budget, deploy or start,
and writes only its lane until he answers.

1. **Pin the census.** Record his request word for word, the commit
   and any uncommitted source. List by ID every open backlog line in
   every section and every line under the wish list's Open, once; a
   narrower scope only on his word.
2. **Check each line at source**: its item file, decision, finding or
   the code it names, by `node tools/find.mjs <ID>`. The line's own
   wording is a lead, no proof. One row each:
   - *state* — still true, partly paid off, paid off or premise
     changed, with evidence (file and line, commit or heading);
   - *blocker* — what holds it now, as checked, or none;
   - *unblocker* — the date, event, gate or word of his that ends it;
   - *size* — under a session, one, two or more, or research; a guess
     with its basis, unknown allowed, never a granted budget;
   - *disposition*, one only — *keep*; *merge* into a named line;
     *narrow* to what; *promote* into the phase; *re-park* on a named
     shelf with its trigger; *retire*, for evidence of payment or a
     concrete reason, dormant being no reason.

   "Not checked" is an answer. Done means the item's criteria, not its
   later pilot or deployment. A compound line: *narrow*, every clause
   given a named home.
3. **Flag** dated work with its date and triggered work with its full
   condition, his action included; a future use does not by itself
   block a repair now. Flag any line whose work would touch a frozen
   tree, a gate, a dependency, a hook, a setting or a host off the
   Network line.
4. **Seal, then a blind pass.** Save this pass and its digest before
   reading another. Brief a second model, as the model policy says
   where one is signed, from his words, the pinned sources including
   uncommitted content, these columns, the lab contract and Always,
   without this pass. Save its answer word for word beside
   its brief.
5. **Compare in writing.** Check each claim it adds at source. One row
   per difference: the line, both answers, the settled answer and why.
   One you cannot settle is his. Agreement proves no claim. Reconcile
   one final disposition row per census ID; the disposition totals
   must equal the census.
6. **Ask once.** The owner's rulings go in one batched question,
   recommendation first, each naming its ID and quoting its governing
   text, the settled lists offered for one yes. Cite existing authority
   instead of asking again; a recommendation or a "maybe" is no start.
   His answer goes in the lane verbatim. Nothing is applied before it.
7. **Apply** what he ruled, reconciling anything that moved since the
   census. A merged, promoted or retired wish leaves the file; a kept
   line stays word for word, closed phase headers too; a narrowed one
   keeps its ID. Trace each removal by ID: "ORIGIN.N — retired —
   reason", "ORIGIN.N — merged into ID — reason". An item retired
   unshipped goes on `**Retired:**`, never the trajectory.
8. **Then the plan verb's step 3**, for the promoted lines ("ORIGIN.N —
   promoted to ITEM-ID — reason"), and on through its steps 4 and 5,
   with its sign-off alternatives. Nothing promoted, no
   phase: one decision records the stock take, its counts and its lane
   by digest. Close by the close verb.
