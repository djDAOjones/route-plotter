# DEF-100 — Path-only video exports draw at the display's size

## Intent

The owner's second Match day MP4 of 2026-10-18, made with Video settings →
Include in export → Background image unticked, was black with the fans small
in its top-left corner. A path-only export hid the background before the
canvas took the export size, and the image's place on the canvas is worked
out again only while there is an image, so the route, its markers and labels,
and every crowd were drawn where they sit on the display canvas. Any
path-only export with a background, at an export size other than the
display's (almost every one), was affected, not only Match day: Site walk's
route is drawn small in the corner, its labels full size and overlapping.

## Acceptance criteria

- A path-only video export draws its route, markers and text labels call for
  call where the same export with its background does, with Camera movement
  on or off.
- In every export frame the image's place, hidden or not, is the image fitted
  to the export size at the background zoom.
- The display after a path-only export is as before: DEF-53's tests pass
  unchanged.
- An MP4 carries no transparency, so a path-only MP4 stays black behind the
  path; that is unchanged here.
- No test is deleted, skipped or weakened.

## Context and sources

- `src/app/exporting.js`: `exportVideo` hides the background, and
  `_enterExportMode` refits the image only while there is one
- `src/services/CoordinateTransform.js`: `setCanvasDimensions` keeps the old
  image place
- `tests/exportFraming.test.js`: the regression tests
- `tests/exportSizeDuringExport.test.js`: DEF-53's path-only frames, checked
  by their calls but not by where the path layer's content sits
