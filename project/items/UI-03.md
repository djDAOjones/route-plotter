# UI-03 — In-place parameter help

## Intent

Parameters need explaining where they are. The owner, 2026-10-05, relayed verbatim by the Backlog status session: "parameters need explaining somehow, ideally in situ i.e. hover over text on question mark, as some hard to know e.g. crowd emitter route bias", and later "I meant release bias". Today a pointer opens a parameter's hint only by a click (keyboard focus opens it too, hover never), although its dotted underline and help cursor suggest hovering; 58 of 133 controls have no hint, all 46 scene-outline fields among them; and some hints use words the panel does not show (Release bias never says which way is earlier). The owner then ordered it in two parts, this first part straight after DEF-77 and ahead of Phase 1's pull requests (relayed: "Next, after DEF-42 (Recommended)", "Two parts (Recommended)"; confirmed in his own chat: "yes, I confirm DEF-77 then UI-03 part 1 next"); the "?" and DEF-14's fix are UI-04, after TST-04.

## Acceptance criteria

- A pointer resting on a parameter's hint text opens its hint after a delay; click, keyboard focus, tap and the screen-reader description work as today.
- A hint opened by hover can be dismissed without moving the pointer, stays open while the pointer is on it, and stays until dismissed or left (WCAG 2.2 content on hover or focus).
- Every unclear hint is reworded in plain language, Release bias first (it says which way is earlier), each in the panel's own words.
- Every parameter control without a hint gets one, the scene outline's fields included.
- The UI standards and digests hold: WCAG 2.2 AAA, Carbon productive; every existing test stays green, none deleted, skipped or weakened.
