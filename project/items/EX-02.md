# EX-02 — Football and riot example projects on the Nottingham aerial

## Intent

The owner asked for "presets for the examples of crowds going to a football stadium and a mob narrative", on the PARM aerial of Nottingham, "included in the current build". He then set the scenes: "the crowd should go to the city ground stands in the south. the mob should go through the square", and "two presets. one for football crowds coming from multiple routes into the four stands. a completely different preset shows a historical mob scenario passing through the square". Two example projects in File → Example projects show what CROWD-08's MVP can do on the client's own map, ready to open, play and adapt.

## Acceptance criteria

- File → Example projects lists Match day and Reform riot, 1831 after the three DEMO-01 examples; each opens from its archive with `images/PARM_Aerial.jpg` as the background.
- Match day: no route; four home crowds fill the City Ground's Trent End, north, east and south stands, and an away crowd fills an away end, each stand a Finish area. Home fans set off from Start areas in the Market Square, at the station and at the bottom edge after Trent Bridge; those from the square and the station walk London Road and Meadow Lane over Lady Bay Bridge. Away fans come from the station and the coach park. Home fans are Okabe-Ito vermillion, away fans blue.
- Reform riot, 1831: no route; a mob in groups enters from the east along Colwick Road, passes through the Market Square and climbs Friar Lane to the Castle. A crowd waits in the square from the start (Final whistle) and sets off as the mob passes; both collect at the Castle in Finish areas.
- Every Finish area seats all its crowd's dots at the render reference, so no place is reused, and every crowd can play, shows dots and finishes on the base timeline.
- Both archives are approved in `public-assets.json` and whitelisted in `.gitignore`, so the build ships them and the release commits them.
- The three DEMO-01 examples are unchanged; no new dependency; every existing test stays green.
