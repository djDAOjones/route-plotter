## Intent

The owner, 2026-10-06: "another to review the layout, interface, and functionality of the crowd features to ensure is useful, usable for novices, depth available for experts, and meets the design criteria".

The crowd features grew item by item, CROWD-01 to CROWD-06 plus DEF-77, UI-03 and UI-04. Each item was checked on its own, but the whole had never been walked as a task, either by a first-time author or by an expert.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Scope:** the crowd cards (Guide, Dots, Release, Motion) and the network cards (Node, Edge), each with its More tier; drawing and tracing a network (pen, junctions, branch and rejoin, one-way edges); the crowd and network rows in Layers and in the scene outline; the CROWD-05 readouts, the Hold at end (CROWD-06), and the crowd hints; crowds in preview, in video export and in the exported player.
- **Usable for novices.** The review walks at least five tasks from a blank project as a first-time author: people walking from one place to another; a crowd that follows the route; a crowd that splits at a junction; a crowd that arrives and stops; a crowd that loops. For each task it records the steps, where the author stalls, and what the author must know that the screen does not show. The target is that each task completes with the primary (always visible) controls, without opening More, and that the defaults give a visible, sensible result.
- **Depth for experts:** every engine parameter that changes what a viewer sees can be reached from the UI, with exact readouts in units; the gaps are listed against `src/models/Emitter.js`, `src/models/FlowLayer.js` and `src/services/SwarmEngine.js`, including the wish-list's multi-emitter authoring and strip drag-reorder.
- **Useful:** each control is checked for a visible effect at its defaults on a typical canvas; controls with no visible effect, two names for one concept, and redundant controls are listed.
- **Meets the design criteria:** `UI-STANDARDS.md` as in UI-06, with weight on the card contract (2–4 primary controls; secondary under More; prerequisites never behind More), the seeded variation rule, recognition over recall (readouts in units and directions), and the hint rules.
- The findings table, the breach/judgement classes, the owner's question round, DEF rows for behaviour defects, the wish-list triage, and the test and screenshot bar all work as in UI-06.
- The review ends with a short note for EX-01 on which crowd examples would teach a novice best. EX-01 stays the owner's to direct.

(c) *Splits at a junction:* Alt+click a major waypoint → place the branch → drag it onto a later major to rejoin → select the crowd → Guide "Custom network" (which drops the author straight into Drawing network mode, replacing the header with a banner) → Done → "Trace route into network" → Edit network → click the fork node → Path weights. About nine actions, four of them unsaid on screen; the traced network is invisible under the route (X-04); the Guide helper says "No network yet" after the trace (X-03). (d) *Arrives and stops* and (e) *loops:* one select, "At journey end", inside the Motion card, which is collapsed by default, so a novice never sees it (X-09); Hold at end, which keeps a loop moving, lives in Route scope.
