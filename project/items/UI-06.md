# UI-06 — Review and tweak the app GUI against the UI standards

## Intent

The owner, 2026-10-06: "please add a task to review and tweak the app gui as per the design requirements". The design requirements are `UI-STANDARDS.md` (Carbon productive, Nielsen's heuristics as hard rules, WCAG 2.2 AAA, and the 13-point design review gate) and the brief's constraints (UoN semantic tokens for chrome, Okabe-Ito for map data).

The last whole-app audits were made against v3.1.473 (February 2026) and v3.1.593 (August 2026). Since then the app has gained crowds, networks, branches, the scene outline, hints, the "?" trigger and the Hold at end. Each item was checked against the gate on its own; the whole had not been reviewed again.

On 2026-10-06 he picked "After Phase 2 (Recommended)" (option text: "New Phase 3, ahead of W4 and the found defects. The reviews see every Phase 2 UI fix, and the tweaks don't clash with them. About 8 items away.") and "Fix breaches, ask on taste (Recommended)" (option text: "The run fixes clear breaches of the UI standards directly (contrast, target size, labels, sentence case). Layout and feature changes come to you as one question round, with screenshots."). Reading those option texts as the placement (Phase 3, ahead of W4 and the found defects) and the method (breaches fixed directly, judgement calls to him as one question round with screenshots) is the filing session's call, for him to confirm. On 2026-10-08 he brought the review half forward, ahead of Phase 2's DEF-20/13/32 ("run UI-06 and CROWD-07, but without modifying the code base, since there is another chat that is modifying files. please do necessary groundwork, analysis, etc, and pause at the point of code editing"); the tweak half waits for his all-clear and for Phase 2's three in-flight defects to land.

## Acceptance criteria

- The review covers every editor surface in Chromium at 1440×900 and 2560×1440: the header and its menus; every left-sidebar card with its More tier; the Layers and Waypoints panel and the scene outline; the splash, the modals, the hint popovers, the export flow, and the exported player's own controls.
- Each surface is checked against the 13 design review gate points, with measured values where a rule has a number: text contrast 7:1 (4.5:1 large); pointer targets 44 × 44 CSS px; visible label matches the accessible name; sentence case; focus order, visibility and non-obscuration; empty, loading, error and success states; reduced motion.
- Every finding in the two earlier audits is marked fixed, still open, or superseded: `_Joe/design docs/UI Audit - Carbon + Nielsen.md` (v3.1.473) and the UI-review write-up (v3.1.593, see sources).
- The findings table lives in this file's Notes. Each row gives the surface, the rule it breaks or strains, the severity, a screenshot reference, the proposed tweak, and a class: **breach** (a hard rule in `UI-STANDARDS.md` is broken) or **judgement** (layout, emphasis, wording, or any feature change).
- Breaches are fixed in this item's PRs without asking.
- Judgement rows go to the owner as one question round with screenshots, and only the rows he picks are made.
- A behaviour defect found on the way becomes its own DEF row under the fix-defects-along-the-way rule. It is not a tweak.
- The review triages the wish-list lines it touches (a triage pass for its scope).
- Every existing test stays green; none is deleted, skipped or weakened. Control goldens that change are regenerated and every changed line is traced.
- The PR shows Chromium before and after screenshots at both sizes.
