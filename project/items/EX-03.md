# EX-03 — Match day upgrade: solid dots and fans from all over

## Intent

The owner, 2026-10-18: "the demos need an upgrade. lets start with the football dots. these need to be more solid (less transparent), and gather at the stadium from all over, such as town square, train station, all directions of transit". EX-02's Match day used dots small enough (0.08) for every fan to have a seat, so they read faintly on the aerial, and its fans came from only four places. This upgrade makes the dots read as solid and brings fans to the City Ground from every direction and kind of transit, while each stand still seats its fans without overlap.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every Match day dot is 0.15, about twice EX-02's, so it reads as solid on the aerial at 1080p; the renderer is unchanged (dots were already opaque fills).
- Each stand's crowd seats as many fans as its Finish area holds at that size, one per seat, and a larger walk-in stream (Disappear) vanishes into the stand, so the streets stay busy.
- Home fans set off from the Market Square, Hockley, Broadmarsh bus station, the station (rail and tram), the north and east edges, Trent Bridge, the Meadow Lane car parks, the ground's car park and a park-and-ride drop-off; away fans from the station and the coach park.
- Crowds wait at pubs on London Road, by the station and by Trent Bridge from the start and leave nearest first (Final whistle); cyclists ride faster along the river and in from the city to bike racks; walkers come from the west edge through the Meadows, and from the station down London Road, to Trent Bridge.
- Every network is one-way and acyclic, every node reaches an Exit, and every Finish area seats all the dots that collect there.
- Only Match day changes; Reform riot, 1831 and the three DEMO-01 examples are byte-for-byte unchanged. No new dependency; no test is deleted, skipped or weakened.
